# ✈️ SkyPulse: Airline Passenger Satisfaction Prediction System
### Kaggle Playground Series – Season 6, Episode 10 (S6E10)
**Primary Evaluation Metric:** Area Under the ROC Curve (**ROC-AUC**)

---

## 📌 1. Project Overview & Problem Statement

Customer satisfaction directly drives brand loyalty, Net Promoter Scores (NPS), and repeat bookings in commercial aviation. However, airlines typically discover passenger dissatisfaction through post-travel surveys after the negative experience has already occurred.

This project implements an **end-to-end Machine Learning decision-support system** based on the official **Kaggle Playground Series (Season 6, Episode 10)** competition dataset. The objective is to predict whether a passenger will report being **Satisfied** or **Dissatisfied** based on flight characteristics, traveler demographics, delay statistics, and 14 detailed inflight/ground touchpoint ratings.

The project is structured with production-grade modularity:
- **Zero Data Leakage:** Preprocessing and scaling are strictly encapsulated in Scikit-Learn `ColumnTransformer` and `Pipeline` architectures.
- **Reproducible ML Engineering:** Fixed seeds, stratified train/validation partitioning, and metric benchmarking across linear, bagging, and boosting paradigms.
- **Interactive Multi-Page Streamlit App:** Real-time single passenger simulation, high-throughput CSV batch inference, interactive EDA dashboard, and model audit interface.
- **Kaggle Official Submission:** Verified alignment with `sample_submission.csv` preserving exact test IDs and probabilities.

## 🔍 3. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Highlights

All EDA plots and metrics are generated by `eda.py` and exported to `outputs/`:

1. **Target Balance:**
   - **Dissatisfied (`False`):** 389,296 records (**55.64%**)
   - **Satisfied (`True`):** 310,339 records (**44.36%**)
   - The dataset is well-balanced; no synthetic oversampling (e.g. SMOTE) is required, avoiding artificial distortion.
2. **Cabin Class Discrepancy:**
   - Over **70% of Business Class** passengers report positive satisfaction, compared to only **~20% in Economy**.
3. **The "Digital Gatekeeper" Touchpoints:**
   - **Online Boarding** exhibits the largest Pearson correlation with satisfaction ($r = +0.560$) and the highest rating gap (**+1.45 points** between satisfied and dissatisfied passengers).
   - **Inflight Entertainment** ($r = +0.431$) and **Inflight Wi-Fi** are the strongest discriminators of passenger loyalty.
4. **Delays vs Cabin Quality:**
   - While Departure Delay and Arrival Delay exhibit high collinearity ($r > 0.95$), operational delays alone account for only minor variance in satisfaction. Cabin hospitality and seat comfort routinely buffer the impact of moderate flight delays.

## ⚙️ 4. Data Preprocessing & Leakage Prevention

Data preprocessing is engineered via Scikit-Learn `ColumnTransformer` and applied exclusively within training folds:

- **Imputation:**
  - `Arrival Delay in Minutes`: Missing values (292 in train, 130 in test) are imputed using `SimpleImputer(strategy='median')`.
  - Categorical features: Guarded using `SimpleImputer(strategy='most_frequent')`.
- **Encoding:**
  - Categorical features (`Gender`, `Customer Type`, `Type of Travel`, `Class`) are encoded via `OneHotEncoder(handle_unknown='ignore', sparse_output=False)`.
- **Scaling:**
  - Continuous and rating features are standardized using `StandardScaler()`.
- **Data Splitting:**
  - Stratified 80% Train / 20% Holdout split (`random_state=42`, `stratify=y`) preserving class ratios.

## 🏆 5. Model Benchmarking & Performance Comparison

Three distinct model families were trained and rigorously evaluated on the 139,927 holdout validation rows:

| Model Architecture | Train Time | Accuracy | Precision | Recall | F1-Score | **ROC-AUC (Primary)** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Logistic Regression** | 2.13s | 0.8781 | 0.8748 | 0.8462 | 0.8603 | 0.9205 |
| **Random Forest** (100 Trees, Depth 18) | 15.26s | 0.9241 | 0.9266 | 0.9003 | 0.9133 | 0.9561 |
| **Hist Gradient Boosting** (Best) | **5.17s** | **0.9238** | **0.9261** | **0.9000** | **0.9129** | **0.9568** |

### Why Hist Gradient Boosting is the Winning Model:
1. **Superior Competition Metric:** Achieves the highest ROC-AUC (**0.9568**), directly matching the official Kaggle S6E10 evaluation objective.
2. **Computational Efficiency:** Leverages integer histogram binning (comparable to LightGBM), fitting over 559,000 samples in **5.17 seconds**.
3. **Compact Production Footprint:** The serialized model artifact is under 5 MB, enabling near-instantaneous startup in the Streamlit web application.

## 💡 6. Feature Importance & Interpretability

Permutation feature importance was evaluated on holdout validation data (measuring decrease in ROC-AUC upon shuffling):

1. **Type of Travel** (ROC-AUC drop: `0.0682`): Business vs. Personal travel is the strongest macro-segmentation factor.
2. **Inflight Wi-Fi Service** (ROC-AUC drop: `0.0443`): Digital connectivity is paramount for modern travelers.
3. **Customer Type** (ROC-AUC drop: `0.0284`): Long-term loyal customers have distinctly different tolerance thresholds.
4. **Online Boarding** (ROC-AUC drop: `0.0097`): Seamless digital check-in sets the tone for the entire customer journey.
5. **Class** (ROC-AUC drop: `0.0054`): Seat pitch and priority cabin perks.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Ethical & Scientific Disclaimer:** These features demonstrate strong statistical associations with reported satisfaction. However, in observational survey data, correlation does not prove causality. Interventions (e.g. upgrading Wi-Fi infrastructure) should be verified through controlled A/B testing.

## 🚀 8. Quickstart & Execution Guide

### Prerequisites
Ensure Python 3.10+ is available. The verified environment uses Python 3.12 with all dependencies pre-installed.

```bash
# Verify Python version
python3 --version
```

Generates high-resolution visualizations in `outputs/`:
```bash

```

### Step 2: Train and Benchmark Models
Trains Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and Hist Gradient Boosting, exports metrics, saves the best pipeline to `models/`, and produces `submission.csv`:
```bash
python3 train.py
```

### Step 3: Test Reusable Prediction Engine
Runs single inference and batch prediction verification on test samples:
```bash
python3 predict.py
```

### Step 4: Launch the Streamlit Web Application
Starts the interactive user interface:
```bash
streamlit run app.py
```
*Access the application in your browser at `http://localhost:8501`.*

## 🎓 10. College Viva & Technical Defense Notes

| Common Viva Question | Recommended Technical Defense |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Q1: Why did you choose ROC-AUC over Accuracy as the selection metric?** | **A:** Kaggle Playground Series S6E10 explicitly evaluates submissions using Area Under the ROC Curve. ROC-AUC evaluates the ranking capability of predicted probabilities across all classification thresholds, making it immune to arbitrary 0.5 decision cutoff bias. |
| **Q2: How was data leakage prevented?** | **A:** Data preprocessing (median imputation of delays, standard scaling, and one-hot encoding) was encapsulated in a Scikit-Learn `ColumnTransformer` inside a `Pipeline`. Preprocessing statistics were fit strictly on the 80% training partition and applied without refitting to the validation and test sets. |
| **Q3: Why Hist Gradient Boosting instead of standard Random Forest?** | **A:** Hist Gradient Boosting discretizes continuous numeric features into integer bins (histogram-based), reducing split-finding complexity from $\mathcal{O}(N \log N)$ to $\mathcal{O}(\text{bins})$. It achieved higher ROC-AUC (0.9568 vs 0.9561) in one-third of the training time (5.17s vs 15.26s). |
| **Q4: How did you handle missing values?** | **A:** In the dataset, only `Arrival Delay in Minutes` had missing entries (292 in train, 130 in test). Because flight delay distributions are heavily right-skewed, a `SimpleImputer(strategy='median')` was utilized instead of mean imputation. |
| **Q5: What are the primary business insights?** | **A:** Digital touchpoints (Online Boarding and Inflight Wi-Fi) show higher correlation and feature importance than physical flight delays. Airlines can dramatically improve customer retention by modernizing online check-in and connectivity. |
